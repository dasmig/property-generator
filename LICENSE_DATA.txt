Data sources of property-generator. The code is under LICENSE.MIT; this file covers resources/.
The tables hold shares, odds and fitted parameters derived from these sources, not their records
or texts. Per-table detail is in doc/data.md; each row of the full tier names its source and year.
Where a source's terms were not found or were not read, this file says so: the owner chose on
2026-11-07 to use those sources and to settle their terms separately (design decisions 12 and 13).

Eurostat: EU-SILC tables ilc_lvho01, ilc_lvho02, ilc_lvho03, ilc_lvho28 and ilc_lvho32, and the
2021 census tables cens_21dwbnr_r3 and cens_21dwop_r3. Source: Eurostat. Eurostat's copyright
notice allows reuse, commercial or not, with the source acknowledged.
https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/about-us/policies/copyright

US Census Bureau: American Community Survey 2023 1-year PUMS, and the American Housing Survey 2023
national public use file. Works of the US federal government, public domain.

UN Statistics Division: Compendium of Housing Statistics 2011, tables 2, 3 and 15 (tenure, rooms,
informal share for the countries listed in the full tier). The un.org terms of use, as read,
allow personal, non-commercial use and forbid redistribution and derivative works without
permission. Used by the owner's decision of 2026-11-07; the terms are the owner's to settle.
https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/sconcerns/housing/

The DHS Program (ICF): indicator HC_RMSL (rooms used for sleeping) through the DHS Program API,
https://api.dhsprogram.com. The API is described as open access, with citation; no reuse licence
for the indicators was found. The DHS dataset terms, which bar redistributing microdata, concern
microdata, which is not used. Cite as: The DHS Program STATcompiler, ICF. Used by the owner's
decision of 2026-11-07.

United Nations, SDG indicator 11.1.1, series EN_LND_SLUM (UN-Habitat), through the UN SDG Global
Database API. The SDG database's terms were not found; UNdata's terms allow copying and
redistribution with the source cited. https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal

The World Bank: World Development Indicators, SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS (from UN World Urbanization
Prospects). CC BY 4.0. https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS

European Commission, Joint Research Centre: GHS-DUC R2023A (GHSL degree of urbanisation by
country), from the GHSL Data Package 2023. CC BY 4.0.
https://ghsl.jrc.ec.europa.eu/

OECD: Income Distribution Database (DF_IDD, INC_DISP and TAC). OECD data are published under
CC BY 4.0 since 2024; the terms page itself refused the download script and was not read.
https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/income-and-wealth-distribution-database.html

United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division (2024): World
Population Prospects 2024, growth rates by year, through the data of the wpp2024 R package
(https://github.com/PPgp/wpp2024). WPP is CC BY 3.0 IGO.

National statistical offices, by country (only shares derived from the tables are shipped):

- China: National Bureau of Statistics, 2020 Population Census yearbook, tables 9-1 to 9-5.
  Terms not found.
- Japan: Statistics Bureau of Japan, 2023 Housing and Land Survey, through the Japan Statistical
  Yearbook 2026, chapter 21. e-Stat's terms allow use, commercial included, with the source cited.
  Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, Japan Statistical Yearbook 2026.
- Brazil: IBGE, Censo Demográfico 2022, SIDRA tables 9880 and 9932. Reuse, commercial included,
  with the source cited (as read in secondary sources quoting IBGE's open-data terms).
  Fonte: IBGE, Censo Demográfico 2022.
- Mexico: INEGI, Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020, tabulados del cuestionario básico.
  INEGI's Términos de Libre Uso allow copying and commercial use, with the source cited.
  Fuente: INEGI, Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020.
- Pakistan: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, 2023 Census, tables 25 and 26, read from the data of
  the CRAN package PakPC2023 (https://cran.r-project.org/package=PakPC2023). PBS's terms not found.
- Philippines: Philippine Statistics Authority, 2020 Census of Population and Housing, OpenSTAT
  tables 0041A6DTOB0, 0061A6DFAO0 and 0071A6DTSH0. Free reuse and redistribution with
  attribution. Source: Philippine Statistics Authority.
- India: National Statistical Office, NSS Report No. 584, Drinking Water, Sanitation, Hygiene
  and Housing Condition in India (76th round, July-December 2018). Government Open Data License -
  India (data.gov.in) allows use, commercial included, with attribution; the ministry site's own
  terms were not read.
- Nigeria: National Bureau of Statistics, Nigeria Living Standards Survey 2018-2019 (case counts
  from its microdata catalogue's variable pages). Terms not found.
- Ghana: Ghana Statistical Service, 2021 Population and Housing Census, 10% housing sample (case
  counts from its microdata catalogue's variable page). Terms not found.
- Egypt: CAPMAS, General Census for Population, Housing and Establishments 2017, household file
  (case counts through the Economic Research Forum's data portal). Terms not found.
- South Africa: Statistics South Africa, General Household Survey 2023 (published shares), with
  DataFirst's ghs-2023-hhold-v1 file for the split of formal dwellings. Stats SA's terms allow use
  with Stats SA credited, and forbid selling the data or reprocessed versions of it without
  permission.

relationship-generator, households.tsv (household types and sizes, from the UN Database on
Household Size and Composition, UN DESA Population Division): see that library's data notes.

country-generator (population, currency, minor unit, subregion, income level): see that
library's LICENSE_DATA.txt.

occupation-generator, income.tsv (median gross earnings by country, from ILOSTAT, or GDP per
head from the World Bank where there is no survey), for each country's reference income in
costs: see that library's LICENSE_DATA.txt.
